Make a Payment

Pay your Rozi Accounting Services invoice online in a couple of minutes. Have your invoice number and amount ready.

Pay your invoice

How it works

Paying online takes three steps.

Find your invoice

Your invoice number and amount due are at the top of the invoice we sent you.

Enter your details

Click Pay now, then enter your invoice number, the amount, and your payment details on the secure page.

Keep your receipt

After your payment goes through, keep the confirmation for your records. We'll mark your invoice as paid.

Paying safely

A few simple rules keep your payment information protected.

  • Only pay through the Pay now button on this page or a link on an invoice from us.
  • We will never ask you to send card or bank account numbers by email or text.
  • If you get a payment request that doesn't look right, call us at (805) 919-6009 before paying.
  • Need to send documents instead? Use our secure client portal.
