Checklist

What to upload

Not sure what you need? Start with what applies to you below. We'll let you know if anything else is needed.

Individuals

  • W-2s from every employer
  • 1099s (interest, dividends, contract work, retirement)
  • 1098 mortgage interest statement
  • Last year's tax return
  • Childcare, education and medical expense records
  • Any IRS or state letters

Businesses

  • Bank and credit card statements
  • Profit and loss or bookkeeping reports
  • 1099s you received and issued
  • Receipts for major purchases and equipment
  • Loan statements
  • Prior-year business return

Keeping your information safe

Your documents contain some of your most sensitive information, so we keep them out of email.

  • Please don't email tax documents, Social Security numbers or bank account numbers.
  • Only upload through the portal link on this page, or the invitation we sent you.
  • We will never ask for your portal password by phone, text or email.
  • If you get a message asking for documents that doesn't look right, call us at (805) 919-6009 before responding.
