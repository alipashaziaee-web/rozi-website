About Rozi Accounting Services

We are a trusted accounting firm dedicated to providing comprehensive financial services to help your business thrive.

Rozi Accounting Services is a small, locally owned Santa Barbara business, started in 2025. As a local firm, we work with our neighbors in the community and give every client the personal attention you'd expect from someone right here in town.

Our mission

At Rozi Accounting Services, we believe that every business deserves access to professional, reliable accounting services.

Our mission is to provide accurate, timely, and cost-effective accounting solutions that help small and medium businesses succeed.

Why choose us?

  • Expert knowledge in accounting and tax regulations
  • Personalized service tailored to your business needs
  • Competitive pricing with transparent fees
  • Committed to accuracy and confidentiality

Meet the owner

Alipasha Ziaee

Founder & Owner

Alipasha Ziaee started his career in audit, where he learned to look at a company's books the way an auditor does: carefully, line by line, with nothing taken for granted. Over the past 10 years he has prepared financial statements for businesses of many kinds, and that experience is at the heart of how Rozi works today.

In 2025 he founded Rozi Accounting Services in Santa Barbara to bring that same level of care to the individuals and small businesses in our community. His audit background means your records are prepared to hold up to scrutiny, whether that's a lender reviewing your statements, an auditor checking your numbers, or you simply wanting to understand where your business stands.
